The hidden problem

On paper the site looked healthy: it ranked over 1,600 keywords and scored 97 out of 100 on-page. But the sitemap declared only 32 URLs while the site actually had 226. Google was being told most of the site did not exist.

of the company's own pages were invisible to Google
Not from missing content. The map that tells Google what exists was pointing at a fraction of it.
The other leaks

The site had zero structured data across every page, while competitors had it. And it ranked #4 for its most valuable search term but was missing from the local map pack, which drives three to five times the leads of an organic listing. That one gap was the largest revenue lever in the engagement.

The part most audits get wrong

The crawl flagged 30+ types of issues. Most SEO reports hand the client that whole list and call it thorough. I triaged it instead.

Fixed first — 80% of the impact
  • Rebuilt the sitemap to include all 226 pages

  • Deployed LocalBusiness, Service and FAQ schema
  • One template fix that resolved 13 blog issues at once
  • Trimmed titles on the top ranking pages
Deliberately skipped — noise
  • Render-blocking flags on a 97/100 site
