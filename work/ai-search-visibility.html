AI Search Visibility

Are you invisible to ChatGPT?

Buyers now ask AI assistants who to hire before they ever open Google. If your brand is not in that answer, you never make the shortlist. Most people selling this hand you one screenshot. That proves nothing.

Applied for a B2B IT services firm, a staffing company, and live prospects across home services.

The problem with a screenshot

These models do not return a ranking. They return a sample. Ask ChatGPT the same buying question twice and you can get two different answers. So a report built on a single check is not a measurement, it is one roll of the dice presented as a fact. The moment your client tries it themselves and sees something different, the whole report loses credibility.

What I built

I wrote my own measurement tool in Python because the commercial ones were selling screenshots. It runs the real questions a buyer types, 25 times per question, per engine, across ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, and reports visibility as a percentage with a 95% confidence interval. When the number moves, you know it actually moved and did not just land on a different roll.

On a live home-services prospect I ran the same test across three cities. The brand showed up in 4 of 9 searches, never in the top three, and in zero of the pet-turf searches, even though the company sells exactly that. The machines simply did not know it. Those buyers went entirely to competitors.

Why it matters now

The shift

The fix

Schema and entity clarity, llms.txt, AI crawler access, and structured answers the models can quote with confidence.

The proof

The baseline is the starting line. Re-measuring the same way is how you prove the work moved anything.
