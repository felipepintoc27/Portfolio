Paid media

Google Ads and Meta

I run Search, Performance Max, Demand Gen, YouTube and Display retargeting. On the largest account I rebuilt all nine campaigns from scratch through the Google Ads API, including remarketing audiences and geographic targeting down to specific communities rather than a radius.

The part I care about most is measurement. Before optimizing anything I check what the account is counting as a conversion, because an account optimizing toward the wrong signal will look healthy and produce nothing. That is how I found an account reporting thousands of conversions where not one was a call or a form.
